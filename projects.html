  • FT-991A Web CAT Controller Control a Yaesu FT-991A from a browser. HTML Ham radio

    A small Flask server talks to the radio over its USB CAT port, and a web page gives you the controls: frequency and band presets, mode, RF power, squelch, PTT and the antenna tuner. It can also stream receive and transmit audio through the browser, so the radio can be operated from another room on the same network.

  • HomeLab Checkup Tool A small terminal tool for checking devices on a network. Python Home lab

    Give it a list of the devices on your network and it pings each one, then shows a color-coded table of what's online and what's down. It runs in the terminal with an arrow-key menu and works on Linux, macOS and Windows.

    View on GitHub →
  • EarthMC Tracker Track players, towns and nations on the EarthMC Minecraft server. Python Minecraft

    EarthMC is a large Minecraft server played on a map of the Earth. The tracker reads the server's public map data to follow player activity and the rise and fall of towns and nations, keeps a history of it, and can post notable events to a Discord webhook.

    View on GitHub →
  • ChessVision Play Stockfish on a real chessboard, watched through a webcam. Python Computer vision

    Point a webcam (or a phone camera) at your board and play against Stockfish with real pieces. The app works out which move you made by seeing which squares changed, then shows the engine's reply with an arrow on the camera image and says it out loud, and waits for you to make that move for it. It copes with shadows, lamps and flickering lights, and there's an undo and a resync button for when things get mixed up.

    View on GitHub →
  • WallServer Drag a file onto your PC and it appears on the TV. Python Home lab

    Turns a TV into a shared wall for your home network. A small server on the machine connected to the TV shows the wall fullscreen in a browser, and a drag-and-drop window on your PC sends it pictures, videos, PDFs and text files, with a live progress card on the TV while they arrive. The wall can show up to four things at once, slots can be pinned, the screen can be blacked out, and there's a control page for your phone.

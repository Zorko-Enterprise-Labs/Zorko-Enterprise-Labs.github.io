About

Zorko Enterprise Labs is a small group of friends who like to make small projects and share them with the world. We're not a company; we're just hobbyists who like to tinker with technology and learn new things.

Donations

Donating is completely optional. Nothing here is locked behind it, and whether you give and how much is entirely up to you.

If you do chip in, the money goes toward parts for our physical projects (the ones that are more than just scripts) and toward keeping this site up. The site itself is hosted for free on GitHub Pages, so that part is really just the domain name.
